Design Exploration & Architecture

Meal Form Refinement: Resolving the 25-Lozenge Wall & Amount Scale Ergonomics

A deep-dive comparative analysis of alternative interaction models for the meal logging form (Form 04). Explores radical redesigns of the 1–5 amount scale and replaces the visual clutter of 25+ unorganized pill lozenges with clear visual hierarchy, cognitive grouping, and safety signal isolation.

1. 1–5 Amount Eaten Scale: Design Alternatives

Design Pattern Visual Metaphor Caregiver Ergonomics Tradeoffs & Suitability
Baseline: 5 Check Discs Discrete checkmark circles (1..5) Requires counting circles; feels like a multi-item checklist rather than volume. Low clarity: doesn't communicate progressive volume; checkmarks semantically mean "done" not "volume eaten".
Variant A: Connected Stepped Fullness Bar Continuous horizontal tank with progressive fill segments (Taste, Little, Half, Most, All) One-tap direct selection; progressive highlight from left to right mirrors plate consumption. Recommended for MVP: Extremely compact (34px height), zero learning curve, clear fractions & labels, seamless in React Native.
Variant B: Interactive Toddler Plate Graphic Circular toddler plate with 5 portion wedges (conic fill 10%–100%) Immediate visceral match with the physical plate sitting in front of the caregiver. Tap plate to cycle. Highest Delight: Playful visual feedback; requires slightly more vertical space (68px diameter + label column).
Variant C: Micro-Bowl Fill Stepper Iconographic bowl silhouettes at 5 fill heights with fraction badges Ideal for bowls, purees, soups, and spoon-fed meals; distinct icon for each step. High Precision: Excellent for baby-led weaning and early solids, though slightly smaller tap targets on small phones.

2. Lozenge Reduction: Categories, Allergens & Textures

Element Group Baseline Problem (Pill Wall) Refined Pattern UX Impact & Safety Benefit
Food Categories (8 items) 8 flat chips mixed together with allergens and textures, creating reading fatigue. 4×2 Bento Grid (Variant A) with bespoke stroke icons
or Macro Compartments (Variant B) (Produce, Staples, Proteins) 		Replaces text parsing with instant icon recognition; structured 2-row layout preserves vertical rhythm and eliminates wrapping wrap artifacts.
Big-9 Allergens (9 items) Allergens look identical to routine food categories. High-stakes safety warnings get lost in visual noise. Safety Shield Drawer (Variant A): Collapsed when 0 flagged; expands to clay warning matrix.
or Tag Tray (Variant B) / Alert Card (Variant C) 		Critical Safety Win: Eliminates warning fatigue. 95% of routine meals have 0 allergen alerts and show zero clutter. Flagged items stand out in Clay warning tint.
Texture & Feeding Style (4 items) Treated as generic multi-select chips, confusing mutually exclusive options with additive flags. Segmented Control Rails (Variant A)
or 2-Column Split Matrix (Variant B) 		Enforces clear single-select radio semantics for texture and feeding style; visually distinct from multi-select food tags.
Synthesis & Production Recommendation

Primary Recommendation: Variant A (Bento Grid & Safety Shield) combined with elements of Variant B. The Stepped Fullness Bar gives immediate portion volume understanding in 34px of height. The 4×2 Bento Grid leverages our 14-stroke Keepsake icon set for rapid scanning. Most importantly, isolating Big-9 allergens into a collapsible Safety Shield Drawer resolves the dangerous warning-blindness created by the baseline form.
