What does “consistent timestamp editing” mean?

The intended behavior is the same editable “When” date and time on every logging and editing form: default to now for a new entry, retain the recorded time when editing, and allow correcting it or logging something that happened earlier. This is when the event happened, not when you saved the correction.

Sleep needs start and end times, with end strictly after start. Saving a corrected time should update timeline order and day grouping, “last feed/diaper/meal” summaries, sleep duration, and introduction watch windows. The entry keeps its identity; saving without changing its time must not move it to now.

Try it: find “When” at the bottom of any form, just before Save. Log an entry with an earlier time, then open it from Latest or Timeline → Edit and correct the time. Sleep has separate start/end fields. Introduction uses the same picker instead of rough offsets, and “Also log a meal” carries its time into the meal form. All times use your device’s local timezone. All demo data is in memory; reloading resets it.
